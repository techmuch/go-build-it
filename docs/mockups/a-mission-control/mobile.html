Mobile idea: a compact version of the console. A bottom tab bar gives Projects · Inbox · Sessions · More. "Inbox" combines questions, reviews and gates (FR-35). Screens use dense lists and colored status chips, the same visual language as desktop.

Rev 2: 🎙 dictation on every answer box · attachments and mockups open full screen (M11) · review rebuilt as an assessment (M5) · several reviewers per rehearsal (M6) · sessions show compaction by default, with handoffs experimental (M8) · new terminal view with a key bar and attach (M10).

Look for: can you answer a blocking question, approve a handoff, and give feedback on a rehearsal step one-handed, in under a minute each?
