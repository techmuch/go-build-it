Mobile idea: the boards and timelines reflow into vertical forms. Lifecycle columns become collapsible sections, and the roadmap becomes a top-to-bottom timeline. A floating dock has a center ⌘ button that opens the command palette, which is quicker than digging through tabs.

Rev 2: attachments and frames open full size · 🎙 dictation · review rebuilt as mockup vs built plus a trace (M5) · several reviewers in a rehearsal (M6) · relay shows compaction by default, batons experimental, and a terminal view (M8).

Look for: do the visual metaphors (stages, timeline, relay, filmstrip) still read well at phone width? Is the command palette a good primary navigation on a phone?
