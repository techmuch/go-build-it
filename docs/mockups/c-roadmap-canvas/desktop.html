Approach C: Roadmap Canvas Desktop · 14 views · rev 2 ← Overview & review guide Approach C mobile → A: Mission Control B: Team Room

Idea: show the work as a picture. Projects move across a lifecycle board. A project's home is its roadmap timeline with gates. Sessions are relay lanes over time. Decisions are a branching tree. Standards and the team are matrices. Navigation is a slim icon rail plus a ⌘K command palette, which suits keyboard users and the CLI-parity goal.

Best for: long-running projects where the hard part is seeing progress, dependencies and how decisions led to the current state. Risk: the visual views take the most effort to build, and the dark, dense styling won't suit everyone.

Rev 2 (feedback from the Approach A review, applied here too): trace-map documents, standards and personas get Edit with an agent chat and a rich editor (03, 10, 11) · attachments and mockups open full size (04, 07, 08) · 🎙 dictation · relay lanes show compaction as the default, with batons (handoffs) experimental, plus a terminal view (05) · review rebuilt as mockup vs built plus a trace of tests, standards, feedback and requirements (09) · swimlane dots open event detail (12) · git workflow moved to standards, plus an experimental section (13).
