Approach B: Team Room Mobile · 9 screens · rev 2 ← Overview & review guide Approach B desktop A mobile C mobile

Mobile idea: works like a messaging app. Tabs are Desk · Rooms · Team · More. The Desk is a stack of cards you work through one at a time, and rooms are chat threads with tap-to-answer replies. Big rounded buttons sit within thumb reach.

Rev 2: attachments and previews open full size · 🎤 dictation everywhere you write · show & tell rebuilt as an assessment (M5) · several people in a rehearsal (M6) · compaction by default, letters experimental, and a terminal peek (M8).

Look for: does the chat model make quick replies feel natural on a phone? Can you still find a project's status without scrolling the conversation?
