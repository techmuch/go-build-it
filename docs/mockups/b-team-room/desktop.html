Approach B: Team Room Desktop · 14 views · rev 2 ← Overview & review guide Approach B mobile → A: Mission Control C: Roadmap Canvas

Idea: working with go-build-it should feel like working with a team (line 18 of the original brief). Each project is a room where personas post updates, ask questions and present work as messages. The home page pairs a "Waiting on you" desk with the project rooms. The tone is warm and editorial, with typography that's pleasant to read for long periods.

Best for: fast back-and-forth with the team, and understanding why things happened. Risk: chat can bury status. Every room therefore keeps a fixed status panel on the right, and the catalog stays on the home page (FR-40).

Rev 2 (feedback from the Approach A review, applied here too): notebook, handbook and persona profiles can be edited by chatting with an agent or directly in a rich editor (03, 10, 11) · attachments and mockups open full size (04, 07, 08) · 🎙 dictation · compaction is the default and handoff letters are experimental; sessions can be watched in a terminal (05) · show & tell rebuilt as an assessment: mockup vs built, tests, standards, feedback, requirements (09) · journal entries open detail pages (12) · git workflow moved into the handbook (13).
