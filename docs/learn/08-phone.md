# gbi on your phone

The phone UI at `/m` is built for what you do away from your desk: answer questions, review work, approve gates and check on members. Reach it over Tailscale (see *Access*).

## Step: Open the phone UI
check: visit /m
show: /m
Open `/m` (on a phone, or narrow your browser window). **Projects**, **Inbox**, **Members** and **More** are the four tabs.

## Step: Find Learn on the phone
check: visit /m/learn
show: /m/more
Open **More → Learn**. Lessons and your progress are the same as on the desktop.

> **Pro tip:** add `/m` to your home screen: it opens like an app, and questions that block work are always first in the Inbox.
