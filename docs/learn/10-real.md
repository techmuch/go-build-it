# Settings, updates and a real project

You have seen every part of go-build-it in the practice project. This lesson covers the settings that matter, updates, and moving on to a real repository.

## Step: Look at the settings
check: visit /settings
show: /settings
Open **Settings** (`g ,`): harnesses and their models, how many members may work at once, auto-dispatch, access over Tailscale, and herdr.

## Step: Open the change log
check: visit /changelog
show: /changelog
Click the version at the bottom of the sidebar (or `g v`). The change log shows what each version changed; **Check again** looks for a newer one and **Update now** installs it, step by step.

> **Pro tip:** updating does not stop your members: herdr keeps them running while go-build-it restarts.

## Step: Import a real project
check: event project.imported any
show: / [data-tour=import-project]
On **Projects**, press **+ Import project** and choose one of your git repositories. go-build-it adds a starter team using your harnesses.

## Step: Reset or remove the practice project
show: /learn
When you are done practising, **Reset** the practice project on **Learn** to start over, or **Remove** it. Your real projects are not affected. Tick this step off when you are ready.
